Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

This policy applies to the apps, software, and websites published by Cameron W. McCann under the name "cwmccann.pro" (together, "the Services"), including the Android apps GPS Bridge and GPS Bridge Trial and the website cwmccann.pro. It explains what is accessed, what is collected, and what is not.

Our approach

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not run advertising, and we do not use advertising identifiers.
  • Our apps do not require an account, and we do not operate servers that receive your app data.
  • Data an app handles on your device stays on your device unless a specific feature below says otherwise.

GPS Bridge and GPS Bridge Trial

These apps relay a GPS position from another device you control (typically your phone) to your Android device over your own local Wi-Fi network and feed it into Android's location system so map apps can use it.

  • Location: the location permission is used to supply received GPS coordinates through Android's mock-location and Fused Location Provider APIs. This data never leaves your device through our apps. It is not sent to us, to analytics services, or to third parties.
  • Background location: used only, if you enable it, to auto-start the bridge when the device reboots (full version only). It is never uploaded.
  • Network: the apps listen on, or connect to, an address you configure, normally your phone's hotspot or home Wi-Fi, to receive NMEA GPS sentences. The connection is direct, device to device, on your local network.
  • Other permissions: notifications (to show the ongoing status and a Stop action), network/Wi-Fi state (to receive data and show this device's local IP during setup), and boot completed (optional auto-start).
  • Purchases: the full version is a paid app sold through Google Play; the Trial app is free. The apps contain no payment code. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play, and we never see your payment details. Google's privacy policy governs that transaction.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting services, ads, or third-party trackers, beyond Google Play Services location.

The website (cwmccann.pro)

  • The site does not use accounts, advertising, or analytics cookies, and we do not run our own user-tracking.
  • Like any web host, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes standard technical request data, such as IP address and browser type, to deliver the site and keep it secure.
  • Some pages embed content from third parties (for example an X/Twitter timeline). Those providers may set cookies or collect data under their own policies when that content loads.
  • If you email us, we receive your email address and message, and use them only to reply.

Future apps and services

Any additional app or service we release is covered by this policy unless it ships with its own. If a new app handles data differently, for example by using a server or an account, we will describe that here or in that app's own policy before release.

Data sharing and retention

We do not sell or share personal information with third parties, except the service providers described above that are needed to run Google Play and the website. Because our apps store nothing off-device, there is nothing for us to retain or delete; uninstalling an app removes its local data.

Children's privacy

Our Services are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.
